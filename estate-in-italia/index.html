Sarah — Un'Estate in Italia · Study Sheet

Free study sheet. No signup. Every question has its answer — try first, then tap.

How to use this
  1. Watch the video once. Don't study — just listen.
  2. Say each answer out loud before you tap. Saying it is what makes it stick.
  3. Every word comes back three times, and gets a little harder each time. That's on purpose.
  4. Do one block, then stop. Come back whenever — just come back.
